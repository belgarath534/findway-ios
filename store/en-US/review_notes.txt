No sign-in is needed. FindWay is made for blind and low vision people and works fully with VoiceOver.

How to try it:
1. Open FindWay and choose Scan my home.
2. Hold the phone up with the back camera facing forward and walk slowly. Spoken tips help you.
3. Choose Mark this spot and type a name, for example Kitchen. Walk to another spot and mark it too.
4. Choose Finish and save.
5. Choose Take me somewhere, pick a place, and follow the spoken directions and the ping sound.

The camera (ARKit) is used only on the device to map the room. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. iPhones with LiDAR also get warnings about obstacles ahead.
